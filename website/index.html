01 / THE ROUTE

One path out.

WireSpot starts Windows Mobile Hotspot from the WireGuard connection profile. Windows then shares that tunnel with your devices.

Windows laptopWi-Fi, Ethernet, or USB uplink
WireGuard tunnelYour VPN connection
Mobile HotspotWindows shares the tunnel
Your devicesPhone, tablet, console

WireSpot verifies the hotspot source and watches for a path around the VPN. If protection fails, its guard stops the hotspot.

02 / BUILT FOR CONTROL

Everything in one place.

Use the desktop app, custom tray menu, or CLI. They share live state and an activity journal.

Go live with confidence

Preflight checks, tunnel verification, and a rollback path if setup fails. Choose balanced hotspot protection or strict VPN-only protection.

Approve devices

New devices wait for your decision before they get internet access. Allow, block, and remember devices from the app or CLI.

Keep DNS on the tunnel

DNS lock and a fail-closed guard help keep hotspot traffic on the intended route as connections change.

Review profiles safely

Import WireGuard .conf files through a review card. Script hooks are rejected, and private keys are never shown in the UI.

Desktop and CLI in sync

Use the window or a command shell with autocomplete. Actions, pause timers, guard state, and device approvals stay aligned.

Fits your routine

Pause and auto-resume, start with Windows without a logon UAC prompt, and uninstall with a choice to keep or delete your data.

03 / DEVICE APPROVAL

You decide who joins.

When approval is on, a new device appears as a request. WireSpot blocks its network access until you allow it by pinning its hotspot IP to a sink MAC address on the laptop.

The block is applied after the device's first packet, so there can be about a one-second window. A device that rejoins with a randomized MAC address asks again. Approval is enforced only while WireSpot runs.

04 / WHAT IT CHANGES

Know what runs.

WireSpot runs as administrator. It creates a WireGuard tunnel service and starts Windows Mobile Hotspot. Its runtime VPN copy has administrator-only access. When you disconnect or uninstall, it removes what it created.

Settings and profiles stay on your PC. The hotspot password is stored in plain text in your local settings file, so use a unique one.

06 / QUESTIONS

Good to know.

Do I need Proton VPN?

WireSpot works with WireGuard profiles, including Proton VPN .conf files. You provide the VPN connection.

Can I use Ethernet or USB for my laptop's internet?

Yes. WireSpot detects the uplink from Windows routing. Your Wi-Fi radio still hosts Mobile Hotspot.

Does it send my VPN keys anywhere?

No. Configuration files stay on your PC. The app's optional exit-IP check contacts ipify through the VPN tunnel.

What happens if the VPN drops?

WireSpot checks the tunnel and hotspot route and stops the hotspot if its guard detects a protection failure while WireSpot is running.
