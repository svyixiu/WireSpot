01 / The route

One path out.

WireSpot starts Windows Mobile Hotspot from a validated VPN connection. Use a WireSpot-managed WireGuard profile or connect in the NordVPN desktop app.

Windows laptopWi-Fi, Ethernet or USB uplink
VPN tunnelA WireGuard profile or the NordVPN app
Mobile HotspotWindows shares the tunnel
Your devicesPhone, tablet, console

WireSpot verifies the hotspot source and watches for a path around the VPN. If protection fails, its guard stops the hotspot.

02 / Built for control

Everything in one place.

Use the desktop app, its tray icon or the CLI. They share live state and one activity journal.

Go live with confidence

Preflight checks, tunnel verification, profile health and a quick speed test. Choose balanced hotspot protection or strict VPN-only protection.

Approve devices

New devices wait for your decision before they get internet access. Allow, block and remember devices from the app, a pop-up or the CLI.

Keep DNS on the tunnel

DNS lock and a fail-closed guard help keep hotspot traffic on the intended route as connections change.

Choose your VPN mode

Import WireGuard .conf files, or enable Profile-less Mode to detect and validate NordVPN. WireSpot never asks for NordVPN credentials.

App and CLI in sync

Use the window or a command shell with autocomplete. Actions, pause timers, guard state and device approvals stay aligned.

Fits your routine

Pause and auto-resume, start with Windows without a logon UAC prompt, pick a theme, and uninstall with a choice to keep or delete your data.

03 / Device approval

You decide who joins.

When approval is on, a new device appears as a request, even while WireSpot is in the tray. WireSpot blocks its network access until you allow it by pinning its hotspot IP to a sink MAC address on the laptop.

The block is applied after the device's first packet, so there can be about a one-second window. A device that rejoins with a randomized MAC address asks again. Approval is enforced only while WireSpot runs.

04 / What it changes

Know what runs.

WireSpot runs as administrator and starts Windows Mobile Hotspot. In profile mode it creates a WireGuard tunnel service. In NordVPN mode it validates the external tunnel and installs forwarding protection; NordVPN remains managed by its own app. WireSpot removes its own changes when hosting stops.

Settings and profiles stay on your PC. The hotspot password is stored in plain text in your local settings file, so use a unique one.

06 / Your VPN profile

Get a Proton .conf.

You can bring a WireGuard profile from any compatible provider. Here's the Proton VPN path, step by step.

  1. Create a Proton VPN account or sign in.Open the Proton VPN account site. WireSpot never asks for your account password.
  2. Go to Downloads → WireGuard configuration.Choose Windows as the platform, give the config a name, and pick the VPN options available on your plan.
  3. Choose a server in the country you want.Proton also offers a recommended server. Click Create, then Download to save the .conf file.
  4. Import it into WireSpot.Open Profiles → Import a .conf, review the details, then choose Go live. Keep the file private: it contains a VPN key.

07 / Questions

Good to know.

Do I need Proton VPN?

No. WireSpot accepts WireGuard .conf profiles from compatible providers and supports an externally connected NordVPN desktop app in Profile-less Mode.

Can I use Ethernet or USB for my laptop's internet?

Yes. WireSpot detects the uplink from Windows routing. Your Wi-Fi radio still hosts Mobile Hotspot.

Does it send my VPN keys anywhere?

No. Configuration files stay on your PC. WireSpot checks its exit IP through ipify while connected. A speed test contacts Cloudflare only when you start it; it sends generated bytes, never your VPN keys.

Can I check a profile before connecting?

Yes. Check resolves and pings its server. Some servers ignore ping, so a missing response doesn't mean the VPN is broken. Once connected, WireSpot can verify a recent WireGuard handshake.

What happens if the VPN drops?

WireSpot monitors the VPN route and stops hosting if protection fails. NordVPN mode also keeps persistent forwarding rules in place until the hotspot is confirmed off.

How do I update?

Download the new WireSpot.exe and run it. A running WireSpot hands over to it, and its installer updates your copy; your settings and profiles stay.
